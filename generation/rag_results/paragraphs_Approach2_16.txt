Because the tumor\nresponse to preoperative chemotherapy has been correlated with resection rate, the improved\nefficacy from the concept that only the patients without K-ras mutations receive an antiEGFR antibody might be expected to increase the conversion rate. The purpose of this study\nis to evaluate the conversion rate from unresectable CRLM to complete resection.\nMethods We conducted a multi-institutional phase II trial for unresectable CRLM. Patients\nreceived mFOLFOX6 with either bevacizumab (bev) or cetuximab (cet) based on K-ras status\n(UMIN000004310).
