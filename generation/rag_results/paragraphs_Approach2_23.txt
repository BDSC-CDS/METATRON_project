    "\nIn summary, the criteria for resectability of colorectal liver\nmetastases have been significantly revised and expanded\nover the course of the last decade. Clinicopathologic factors\nsuch as the tumor number, tumor size, presence of extrahepatic disease, or width of the resection margin should no\nlonger be used to categorically exclude patients from consideration for surgical resection.
